## Contribute and receive credit

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Code, regression tests, documentation, accessibility improvements, reproducible bug reports, and privately reported security findings can all contribute to the project.

Use the name and contact information you intend to make public in commits. Preserve existing authorship and relevant attribution when adapting another contribution. Co-author trailers should identify actual contributors with their agreement. Do not publish another person's private contact details or list an organization as endorsing the project without authorization.

Contributors remain responsible for reviewing tool-assisted work, its provenance, and its verification claims.
